# AI But Simple reference investigation

**Observed:** 2026-11-08, Europe/Istanbul · **Tools:** REA 6.8.0 and public page inspection

AI But Simple offers a weekly visual research briefing, an archive of issues and topic pages, and a separately marketed PRO research application. The public surfaces show how editorial explanations, discovery signals, and access tiers are presented. Private collection, ranking, and review implementation are outside the evidence boundary. [Homepage](https://www.aibutsimple.com/) · [Archive](https://www.aibutsimple.com/archive) · [PRO](https://www.aibutsimple.com/pro)

## How the public product works

| Area | Observation | Design implication for Viam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial cadence | The homepage promises weekly email. The archive showed 125 issues, with #125 on November 2 and #124 on October 26, 2026. Earlier October entries show that actual publication can skip a week. The About page describes a human editorial process. [Homepage](https://www.aibutsimple.com/) · [Archive](https://www.aibutsimple.com/archive) · [About](https://www.aibutsimple.com/about) | Refresh sources on separate schedules. Publish a curated weekly project digest from versioned evidence and show feed health independently of publication cadence. |
| Durable archive | Issues have numbers, dates, topic tags, stable article URLs, and access labels. Search, topic routes, page links, and grid or list views make prior material retrievable. The list view groups entries by year. [Archive](https://www.aibutsimple.com/archive) · [Training topic](https://www.aibutsimple.com/t/training) | Give source revisions, techniques and briefs stable identities. Expose explicit topics and date filters, with stable pagination across refreshes. |
| Explanations and prerequisites | The June 2024 optimizers issue uses diagrams, prerequisites, linked earlier issues, and a discussion of tradeoffs. Issue #122 covers multiple developments with a table of contents, mechanism explanations, figures, primary-paper links, and related entries. [Optimizers issue](https://www.aibutsimple.com/p/optimizers-deep-learning-simple-explanation) · [Issue #122](https://www.aibutsimple.com/p/claude-opus-5-5-agent-swarms-writing-proofs-cheap-sparse-models-1) | Keep a reviewed technique note separate from the dated discovery item. Link prerequisites, implementations, evaluation setup and applicability. Use diagrams where they clarify a mechanism. |
| Discovery and evidence | Issue #122 separates short discovery items with community signals from long-form analysis and links a versioned research source. Issue #125 links a model artifact and says the author inspected configuration, checkpoint and weight index because a technical report was unavailable. The latter is the publisher's description of its method. [Issue #122](https://www.aibutsimple.com/p/claude-opus-5-5-agent-swarms-writing-proofs-cheap-sparse-models-1) · [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37725v1) · [Issue #125](https://www.aibutsimple.com/p/nanbeige4-3-33b-simply-explained) | Treat discovery signals as prioritization inputs. Keep source artifacts and claim-level evidence separate and label the scope of an artifact inspection. |
| Newsletter and app boundary | An older article showed a subscriber sign-in gate and a Beehiiv footer. The live capture fetched public assets from `media.beehiiv.com` and site assets from `www.aibutsimple.com`. Public pages link to a separate app domain for sign-in. [Issue #85](https://www.aibutsimple.com/p/a-deep-dive-into-modern-transformers-optimizing-attention) · [Homepage](https://www.aibutsimple.com/) | Separate publication from the research index and access layer. Neither Beehiiv nor the reference site's application is a Viam runtime dependency. |
| PRO research layer | The landing page describes technique pages, links between papers, models and repositories, role-based signals, search across entity types, ranking refreshed per run, and independent reproductions. These are publisher claims about product capabilities. [PRO](https://www.aibutsimple.com/pro) | Build explicit relationships and a transparent ranking policy for the pilot. Keep an independent reproduction as a distinct reviewed evidence type. |

### What remains unknown

- The archive routes expose the public query interface. The search database, tokenizer, ranking, cache invalidation, and hosting topology remain unknown.
- The PRO page describes a research workflow. The public investigation did not establish its private data sources, ingestion job schedule, ranking weights, use of models, data store, or criteria for certifying a reproduction.
- The account and signup flows were outside the runtime scenario. No sign-in, subscription, purchase, or access to gated content was attempted. Unfinished network traffic and missing initiator stacks limit causality claims.

### Reproduce the public checks

1. Run REA `capture-browser-scenario` with an installed Chromium browser, `mode: launch`, `headless: true`, and no imported storage. Start at the public homepage and wait for `h1`. Navigate to `/archive`, `/p/nanbeige4-3-33b-simply-explained`, and `/pro`, waiting for `h1` on each. Select DOM, URL, and network events with request and response body capture disabled.
2. Start a fresh scenario at `/archive`. Wait for `form[role=search] input[name=q]`, fill `optimizers`, and press Enter. Wait for `input[name=q][value=optimizers]`. Click `a[href*="view=list"]`. Inspect the resulting document URL, status and issue links.
3. Read the `archive.js` URL from the archive DOM. Save that one public asset into an otherwise empty directory, hash it, and run `rea analyze-javascript-application <asset-directory> --json`. Record the Evidence ID, observed version parameter and digest. A future deployment can change both the script and the resulting Evidence IDs.

## Transfer to the Cassandra build

Viam uses the public pattern of discovery, explanation, and retrievable history to serve a different workflow. A project should be able to ask which training or architecture claims changed since its last decision and get the applicable source versions and experiments. The [architecture](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) specifies how to retain that evidence. The [requirements and build plan](../specification.md) defines the first collectors, the review states, and the tests for source freshness and comparison.
